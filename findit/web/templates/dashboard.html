      Reading this page

      Monthly change
      Every figure compares a scheme's holdings with the previous month. Stocks no fund bought or sold are not ranked, and a stock with no monthly change is never shown as a buy.
      AMCs net and discretionary net
      AMCs net counts fund houses buying minus those selling; it is the ranking key. Discretionary net counts only AMCs whose change beats the drift that price moves alone would cause. It, new-position flow and the FII/DII changes are under More columns and in each stock's detail.
      Units
      Flows are in ₹ crore. FII and DII changes are percentage points of the company's shares between its two latest quarterly filings.
      Missing, no increase and stale
      missing means no quarterly filing was published by the date the month's portfolios became public. It is never read as zero or as no increase. No increase means two filings exist and the holding did not rise. No prior quarter means only one filing exists, so no change can be measured. stale marks a filing more than 200 days old at that date.
      DII (MF+banks+insurance)
      A conservative aggregate of mutual funds, banks, insurance companies and other financial institutions.
      What this is not
      A record of what funds did, not a buy list. It reports changes in quantity, flow and value, not performance.
