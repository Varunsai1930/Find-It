## Pilot cohort reporting

Use actual counts with denominators at recruitment, activation and each real new release. Freeze the definition and cohort before reporting. Report independent/non-discounted payments separately, settled collections minus refunds separately from revenue, and founder labor separately from cash. Track every loss/decline reason. Small-sample targets are decision aids, not statistical proof.

Do not calculate acquisition conversion from page views alone. Use qualified contacted/consented prospects, enrolled participants, activated participants and paying customers from the same dated channel cohort. Price/retention assumptions in the workbook are assumptions until observed. No market-size number is supplied without an identified, deduplicated source population and role eligibility.

## Update after the pilot

Attach source-review and coverage releases, actual interview/competitor task summaries, payments/refunds, two release cohorts, invoices/time records and the business calculation. Explain failed targets and conflicting observations. Choose continue/change/stop the audience or job before adding unrelated features. Reassess source access, support burden and distribution permissions before promising wider coverage.
